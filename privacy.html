Privacy Policy

How SmartTools handles information and files.

Local processing

Where practical, tools process files in your browser. Files processed locally are not sent to SmartTools servers by the tool itself. Some advanced tools may require an external service; their data handling should be explained on that tool page before use.

Accounts

If account features are enabled, authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. SmartTools does not store your account password directly.

Advertising and analytics

SmartTools may display advertising through Google AdSense after approval. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies. You should update this policy to reflect the exact services enabled on your live site before launch.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to smarttoolsgns@gmail.com.

This starter policy is a draft for the project and is not legal advice. Review it for your jurisdiction and actual third-party services before publishing.
